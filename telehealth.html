Our Services

Telehealth

Noor Health Clinic offers secure telehealth visits by video or telephone to make care more accessible, convenient, and connected.

Book an appointment →

Our nurse practitioner-led team uses virtual visits for concerns that can be safely evaluated without a hands-on examination. Telehealth is part of a hybrid care model: it complements, but does not replace, in-person care when an examination, testing, vaccination, or procedure is needed.

Virtual visits can provide clinical outcomes and patient satisfaction comparable to usual care for many appropriate chronic disease follow-ups.
