Hodo Mohamud, DNP, ARNP

Adult-Gerontology Acute Care Nurse Practitioner

As a co-founder of Noor Health Clinic, Hodo brings more than five years of advanced practice experience in caring for adults with complex cardiovascular and chronic medical conditions. Her work focuses on helping patients navigate the day-to-day realities of conditions such as heart failure,

Philosophy of Care

“Hodo’s care philosophy is centered on clinical vigilance, continuity, and informed partnership. She uses remote monitoring tools—including cellular blood pressure monitors, smart scales, and glucometers—to extend care beyond the visit, identify relevant changes in a patient’s condition, and guide timely follow-up. Her goal is to help patients maintain stability, reduce avoidable acute care needs, and better understand their role in managing complex chronic conditions.”
