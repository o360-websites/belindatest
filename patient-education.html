The Practice

Get to Know Noor Health Clinic

Noor Health Clinic is a Nurse Practitioner-led primary care practice, built on the belief that accessible healthcare is a fundamental right.

Shadia and Hodo are University of Washington-trained Nurse Practitioners who founded Noor Health Clinic to give families un-rushed visits, clear health guidance, and simple home monitoring.
