How Membership Works

Direct Primary Care (DPC) Membership

Our membership model gives you direct access to your primary care provider through longer visits, same-day, communication, and connected care at home – all for a simple monthly fee with no copays or surprise bills. Members also receive secure, HIPAA-compliant messaging with the providers(s) through Spruce Health for convenient support between visits.

Longer Visits

Direct access to your primary care provider.

Same-Day Access

Reach your care team when you need it.

Connected Care at Home

Home devices keep your provider in the loop.

Secure Messaging

HIPAA-compliant messaging through Spruce Health.

Primary care does not replace health insurance, and recommends that patients keep at least a high-deductible insurance plan for hospital care, emergencies and specialist services.
