Our Services

Preventive Care and Wellness

At Noor Health Clinic, wellness is about more than avoiding illness. It is about supporting your physical health, emotional well-being, daily energy, and ability to live in a way that feels meaningful to you.

Book an appointment →

Our nurse practitioner-led team provides personalized, evidence-based preventive care for adults in the Seattle and Bellevue communities, with a focus on helping you feel well now while protecting your health for the future.

We take a whole-person approach that considers your medical and family history, current medications, nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, mental health, culture, relationships, daily responsibilities, and personal goals. We combine this broader understanding of wellness with recommended screenings, immunizations, and medical care based on current evidence.

Your Plan

Wellness Is Personal

There is no one-size-fits-all plan for health. We meet you where you are and work with you to create realistic, sustainable steps that fit your life, values, and resources. Whether your goal is improving energy, managing stress, sleeping better, becoming more active, reducing health risks, or staying ahead of chronic conditions, we focus on practical changes that can be maintained over time.

Our Approach

A Whole-Person, Evidence-Based Approach

Our approach recognizes that health is influenced by the connection between body, mind, environment, and everyday routines. We listen to what matters most to you and use evidence-based preventive recommendations to guide shared decisions about screenings, vaccines, lifestyle support, medications, testing, and referrals.

We do not expect perfection or major changes all at once. Small, consistent steps—such as adding regular movement, improving sleep routines, choosing nourishing foods more often, reducing stress, or monitoring blood pressure at home—can support long-term health and help lower the risk of conditions such as high blood pressure, diabetes, and heart disease.
