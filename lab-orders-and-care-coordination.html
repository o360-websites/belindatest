Lab Partners

Labcorp & Quest Diagnostics

We partner with Labcorp and Quest Diagnostics for laboratory services. For membership patients with insurance, medically necessary laboratory testing is billed through their insurance plan; coverage and any out-of-pocket cost depend on the individual plan and test ordered.
